How to read a result. A green PASS means the entered plate thickness meets the calculated ISO 12216:2020 requirement for that one opening only. It does not confirm every clause of the standard is satisfied — area boundaries, sandwich-glazing interlayer limits, retention/fixing details and escape provisions must still be checked separately. In case of doubt, or a result marked with a caution note, contact Blue Peter Marine before relying on this calculation.
