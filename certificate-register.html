How to publish changes
  1. Add or edit entries above. PDFs you attach are kept safely in this browser until you publish them.
  2. Click Download upload pack (ZIP) and unzip it. It holds certificates.json and a certificates folder with the new PDFs.
  3. On GitHub, open the bluepetermarine.github.io repository, click Add file, then Upload files, and drag in both certificates.json and the certificates folder. Commit the changes.
  4. Within a minute or two, every client sees the updated register under each essential requirement in RCD Navigator.

The register is shared by all clients and is not linked to any one project. Certificates open without a watermark.
