Catamaran GZ Curve

From Marchaj's Sailing Theory and Practice
Key Characteristics:
• GZ max occurs at approximately 12.5° when windward hull just lifts
• Righting moment declines steadily after peak
• Angle of vanishing stability: approximately 70°
• Beyond 70°, the catamaran has zero righting moment and will capsize
• Sharp early peak reflects the wide beam stability of the catamaran design
