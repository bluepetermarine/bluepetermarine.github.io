⚠️ PETROL FUEL SYSTEM SAFETY WARNING

Petrol vapors are highly flammable and explosive. All components must meet strict safety standards for ventilation, bonding, grounding, and fire prevention. Particular attention must be paid to vapor control and electrical safety.
