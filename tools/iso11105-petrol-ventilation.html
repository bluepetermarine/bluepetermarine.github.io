Requirements Summary

Key Requirements from ISO 11105:2020:
• Engine compartments require BOTH natural and powered ventilation
• Tank compartments require natural ventilation only (powered not required)
• Exception: Tank compartments with remaining volume < 3L need no ventilation
• Exhaust ducts must originate in lower one-third of compartment
• Supply and exhaust openings separated by at least 600mm (where compartment dimensions permit)
• Minimum duct cross-section: 3,161 mm² (engine) or 1,134 mm² (tank)
• Blowers must operate for 4 minutes before engine start
• All electrical equipment must be ignition-protected per ISO 8846:1990
• Compartments must be separated from habitable spaces with continuously sealed boundaries
⚠ Important Notes:
• Ventilation ducts must be self-draining
• Terminal fittings for flexible ducts must be ≥ 80% of required duct cross-section
• Ventilation openings (louvers, grates) must meet full cross-sectional area requirement
• Intake and exhaust must be separated to minimize recirculation
• For portable tank storage: compartment must be open to atmosphere or naturally ventilated
