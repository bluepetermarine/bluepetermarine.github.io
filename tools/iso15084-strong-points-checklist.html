Minimum Required Breaking Strength — ISO 15084:2003 Clause 6.2

The values below are the minimum required breaking strengths of each strong point and its surrounding structure, expressed in kN. The standard defines these as the horizontal load Pn that the fitting must withstand without failure or permanent deformation. No additional safety factor is applied on top of these values — the design category coefficient f already provides the appropriate margin for the sea conditions. Lines and chains attached to any strong point must not exceed 80 % of that strong point's breaking strength (Annex A, clause A.1). Where LC = (LH + LWL) / 2.
