Clause 5 — Slip-Resistant Surfaces

All standing/walking surfaces in deck zones must be slip-resistant. Transition bands to appliances/steps may be non-slip-resistant if < 75 mm wide. Glazed appliances ≤ 500×500 mm outside periphery areas may be exempt. Trampolines/nets forming deck zones must also be slip-resistant.

Clause 13 — Means of Reboarding

All craft. Reboarding must be possible unaided.
Rigid ladder: bottom rung ≥ 560 mm below waterline (mLC) | rung spacing ≤ 305 mm | total width ≥ 200 mm (100 mm/foot) | hull clearance ≥ 100 mm | tread depth ≥ 25 mm (slip-resistant) | handhold within 500 mm of top rung | top rung ≤ 500 mm below Z3 deck | strength 1,800 N at any rung
Non-rigid ladder: rigid rungs ≥ 250 mm wide | 2+ attachment points ≥ rung width apart | bottom rung ≥ 1,200 mm below waterline | submerged rungs negative buoyancy
Deployable: activation ≤ 500 mm above waterline | max 100 N activation force | works without engine/power
