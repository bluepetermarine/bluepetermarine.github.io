Understanding Model Year

The model year is the year when the specific craft is intended to be placed on the market. It represents a 12-month period that can extend across two calendar years.

Model Year Examples:
A craft manufactured in November 2025 (coded K5) could have model year 25 or 26, depending on when the manufacturer intends to market it:
• If marketed as a 2025 model: WIN ends with K525
• If marketed as a 2026 model: WIN ends with K526

The manufacturer decides which calendar year is designated as the model year based on their marketing strategy.

Manufacturer Identification Code Registration

⚠ Registration Required for European Market
For craft to be placed on the European market under Directive 2013/53/EU, the Manufacturer Identification Code (MIC) must be registered with a European entity according to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/1 of 3 January 2017.

Key Requirements from ISO 10087:2022:
• WIN consists of 14 consecutive characters plus one hyphen (total 15 characters)
• Country code followed by hyphen (e.g., ZA-)
• Manufacturer code may not contain numerals 0 or 1
• Serial number may not contain letters I, O, or Q
• Month of manufacture date must be no earlier than when construction began and no later than when craft leaves manufacture or is placed on market
• WIN must be visible on starboard side, near stern, within specified locations based on craft type
• Duplicate WIN required in hidden location known only to manufacturer
• Rails, fittings, or accessories must not obscure the identification number
• WIN must be affixed during construction/assembly - craft cannot be marketed without it
