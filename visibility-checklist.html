3. Horizontal field of vision, forward

Measured from the eye position at the main steering position, under normal conditions of use (clause 4.1).

4. Vertical field of vision, forward

Determined with the craft at the attitude of the level reference line in the loaded condition mLDC (ISO 8666). The obstructed distance to the water, measured from the stem or point of visual obstruction, shall not exceed 4 × LH and never more than 50 m (clause 4.2.2.2).

For each sight line, enter the point of visual obstruction (windscreen frame, coaming, bow, etc.): its horizontal distance forward of the eye position and its height above the level reference line. The tool works out where the lowest unobstructed line from the low eye position reaches the water.
